# Olive ethics and safeguarding charter

For school leaders, teachers, nurses, and IT staff. Pupils are children; these rules outrank any feature.

## Commitments
1. **Children first.** Safeguarding disclosures (harm, abuse, bullying, distress) end the AI exchange and direct the pupil to a trusted adult. Olive checks this on the server before any model call; staff must still follow the school's safeguarding policy and record concerns there.
2. **A person decides.** Olive never grades, disciplines, diagnoses, ranks, or places pupils. Nurse cues support, never replace, qualified care and emergency procedures.
3. **Collect the minimum.** No behaviour scoring, emotion/face detection, or covert monitoring. Do not type health records, passwords, or safeguarding details into the front desk.
4. **Be honest about AI.** Replies are labelled. Simulated and connected data stay visibly separate. Never present scripted output as school fact.
5. **Fair and accessible.** Test with different year groups, languages, and abilities. Keep keyboard, screen-reader, and reduced-motion support working.
6. **Learning stays the pupil's.** The tutor asks and hints; it does not hand over finished homework. Teachers can pause it.
7. **Open to review.** Publish what Olive does and keeps. Provide a route for parents and staff to question, object, or request deletion.

## Gate before any model training
- [ ] Written approval from the safeguarding lead and data protection officer
- [ ] Documented lawful basis and parent/guardian notice (and pupil-friendly explanation)
- [ ] Data de-identified; no medical, safeguarding, or disciplinary records
- [ ] Training data sources and licences recorded
- [ ] Bias and harm evaluation across pupil groups, repeated after each retrain
- [ ] Red-team tests for self-harm, bullying, grooming, and age-inappropriate content
- [ ] Retention period, deletion process, and withdrawal route defined
- [ ] Staff briefed; a named owner can switch Olive off immediately

## Before real pupil data
Add real sign-in and per-user authorization, HTTPS, safe request logging, and an incident process. The persona picker is a demo, not access control.
